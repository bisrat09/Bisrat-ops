## Reading, run 2 (James, 2026-09-20, after the Publer backfill and the IG views/reach split)

Run 1 earlier tonight had 42 posts with engagement. Publer had the rest back to March: 79 of 103 posts now have numbers. Instagram views (reels only, 36 posts) and Instagram reach (carousels and posts, 75) are separate columns and never mixed.

**Holds on every platform, both metrics**

- Names a major AI CEO or founder (9 to 13 posts): views 1.4x Instagram, 1.5x TikTok, 2.9x YouTube, 7.1x Facebook. Reach 1.3x Instagram, 4.2x Facebook. Lane #1 confirmed on the larger sample.
- An AI doing something no AI had done (6 to 8 posts): views 1.2x Instagram, 5.6x Facebook, flat TikTok. Reach 1.6x Instagram, 3.3x Facebook. Second-strongest positive signal.

**Confirmed drags**

- Medical claims (5 to 10 posts): Facebook views 0.17x, Facebook reach 0.07x, Instagram reach 0.86x. TikTok shows 3.8x on 3 posts, driven by one cancer reel. Drag stands.
- Job-replacement framing (6 to 8 posts): views 0.8x Instagram, 0.85x TikTok, 0.6x Facebook. Facebook reach 0.3x. Drag stands.
- Dollar figure central (5 to 14 posts): Facebook views 0.10x, YouTube 0.5x, Facebook reach 0.10x. Instagram and TikTok flat. Rule as Mike set it: in the story, not the hook.
- Company money as the main event (4 to 6 posts): Facebook views 0.2x, Facebook reach 0.11x. Matches Mike's brand_fit level 1.

**New rows that became readable with the backfill**

- Developing world (3 to 5 posts): Facebook views 0.17x, Instagram reach 0.40x, Facebook reach 0.09x. Down everywhere it can be measured. Small n, but every number points the same way. Worth Mike's attention given the developing-world series is active daily curation.
- Opens with a question (4 to 5 posts): Facebook views 0.18x, Instagram reach 0.38x, Facebook reach 0.10x. Down everywhere.
- Usable today (6 to 17 posts): Instagram views 1.2x but Instagram reach 0.68x, TikTok 0.6x, Facebook views 0.22x. Leans down.
- A named ordinary person (4 posts): Instagram reach 0.38x. Too few to call, but not the lift Mike's brand_fit level 4 assumes. Watch it.

**Payoff-first, now with clean Instagram data.** Caption leads with the outcome: Instagram views 0.68x (19 vs 17 reels), Instagram reach 0.70x (44 vs 31), TikTok 1.4x, YouTube 5.1x, Facebook views 0.36x but Facebook reach 2.35x. Mike asked for a re-test once IG was clean. It is clean now and Instagram is still down on both metrics. Facebook splits by metric. The rule stays as Mike set it, but this is the row to revisit at the next run.

**Still unreadable** (fewer than 3 posts on a side): rivals agreeing (2 posts), AI discovery (2), autonomous agents on most platforms.

**For pitches:** famous faces first, with the numbers above. AI-first as the second lane. Medical, job-replacement, dollar hooks, company money, and question openers are measured drags.

Rerun after each engagement import: `python3 scripts/log_engagement.py --days 30` then `python3 scripts/jev_features.py`. Half a cent.
