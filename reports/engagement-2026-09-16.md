## Patterns

1. **Named power figures plus consensus beats everything.** The two top reels overall are
   Dario Is Right (Amodei, Altman, Musk agree) and Dean Hassabis (two Google leaders step back
   the same day). Both are about specific famous people doing something unexpected together.
2. **YouTube Shorts is the only platform showing breakout behavior.** TikTok, IG, and FB each
   cap around 300 views per reel and have done so for two months. YouTube went 105 to 916 in
   one week. If the goal is reach, YouTube is where the ceiling is not fixed.
3. **Wonder and human-interest stories underperform on YouTube.** Life Began Twice, Wu Sesame,
   Photo Robs You, and Riemann all sit at 1 to 4 YouTube views. On IG they do fine. Platform
   matters more than topic for those.
4. **Ghostwriters is the odd one.** Weakest on IG, TikTok, and FB, but second on YouTube. The
   Nairobi data-labeler angle may have found a different audience there.
5. **Average view duration is short.** Dario held 34s of 102s. AI Civilization held 71s of 143s,
   the best retention, but had a tenth of the views. Length past 100s is not what is holding
   people; the hook is.
6. **Saves and shares are near zero everywhere.** Nothing in this window has been treated as
   worth keeping or passing on. That is the metric that predicts follower growth and it has
   not moved.
